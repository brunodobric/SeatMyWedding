Privacy Policy

SeatMyWedding (web app and iPhone app) · effective 21 October 2026. This is a translation; the Croatian version prevails.

SeatMyWedding helps you plan the seating at your wedding. We collect only what the app needs to work, and we don’t sell data to anyone.

What data we process

  • Wedding data you enter yourself: the title, tables, guest names, groups, notes and the seating plan.
  • Account data, if you sign in: your email address, the name and profile picture provided by Google or Apple, and a unique user ID.

We don’t use analytics, ads, or tracking across other apps or websites.

Where the data is stored

Without signing in, your data stays on your device only. If you sign in, a copy of your wedding data is saved in your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so it is available on all your devices. Each account can read and change only its own data.

Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for signing in and storing data. Privacy in Firebase.
  • Google or Apple, if you sign in with that account.

We don’t share data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Deleting your data and account

You can permanently delete your account and all wedding data in it in the app: Options → Delete account. Data on the device is deleted when you sign out (if you choose so) or when you delete the app.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, transfer and delete your data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Croatia, AZOP). You can download a copy of all your data in the app yourself: Options → Save a backup.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to bruno.dobric@icloud.com.

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
